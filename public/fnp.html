📺 Doctors, Patients & Chaos — Classic Cartoons
The Daffy Doc (1938) — Looney Tunes Daffy as surgical assistant gets thrown out of the OR, then kidnaps Porky Pig to operate on him with a saw. 7 minutes of pure chaos.
Patient Porky (1940) — Looney Tunes Porky checks into a hospital with a stomachache. A fellow patient is impersonating "Dr. Chilled-Air" and is VERY eager to operate.
Hare Tonic (1945) — Bugs Bunny Bugs convinces Elmer Fudd he's infected with "rabbititus" and orchestrates a fake epidemic in Elmer's house. Epidemiology, Bugs-style.
Hospitaliky (1937) — Popeye Popeye AND Bluto both fake illness to get Nurse Olive Oyl's attention. They compete to be the sickest patient. First cartoon where Bluto eats the spinach.
Greedy for Tweety (1957) — Looney Tunes Sylvester, Hector, and Tweety all end up hospitalized. Granny as nurse. Hospital politics at their most unhinged.
Is There a Doctor in the Mouse? (1964) — Tom & Jerry Jerry brews a super-speed potion and Tom has a very bad time. Chuck Jones directing. Medical premise throughout.
Calling Dr. Woodpecker (1963) — Woody Woodpecker Woody delivering flowers to a hospital runs into Nurse Meany and disguises himself as a famous surgeon. Goes exactly as expected.
