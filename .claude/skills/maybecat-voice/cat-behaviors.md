# Cat Behavior Reference — USE INSTEAD OF DEFAULTS

Every AI defaults to the same 12 cat behaviors. Stop. Use these instead.
The alternatives are NOT personality-locked — any cat in any setting could do most of these. The specifics (objects, surfaces, prey, textures) vary based on what's in the cat's world.

---

## INSTEAD OF: Sitting in a sunbeam

The overused version: cat finds a sunbeam, lies in it, warmth, contentment.

Use these instead:
1. Sleeping on top of the cable box / router — warm from running all day, fur clogs the vents
2. Pressed against the side of a laptop charger brick on the floor
3. Lying on the closed lid of a laptop that's still warm from use
4. On the bathroom floor after someone took a hot shower — the steam, the warm tile
5. On top of the dryer mid-cycle — the heat AND the vibration
6. Wedged behind the TV where the warm air vents out
7. On the hood of a car that just parked — engine heat radiating up through metal
8. On a heating vent when the furnace kicks on — the exact moment the air shifts from cold to warm
9. Curled against a sleeping human's back — borrowing body heat through the blanket
10. In a cardboard box with a blanket wadded in it — insulated warmth they built themselves
11. On a stack of paper that came out of the printer warm
12. Lying on black asphalt in a driveway that's been soaking up afternoon heat

**Bonus — weird but real:**
13. Lying on a pizza box that still has warmth from the pizza — not the pizza, the cardboard
14. Pressed into the warm gap between couch cushions where someone was just sitting — the ghost of a lap
15. On top of a closed dishwasher right after it ran — steam rising through the seam, cat melting
16. On a docked roomba — warm flat surface that sometimes moves, doesn't care
17. Curled on the exact spot on the couch you just got up from — claiming your residual warmth before it fades
18. On top of someone's head while they sleep — highest point in the bed, warmest spot in the house

## INSTEAD OF: Ignoring the owner

The overused version: cat is called, doesn't come, is aloof, humans are servants.

Use these instead:
1. Sitting with their back to you but one ear rotated fully backward — tracking you without the dignity of looking
2. Being in the same room as you all day but never closer than four feet — maintaining parallel presence
3. Watching you from on top of the fridge with an expression that could be fondness or surveillance
4. Coming when called... to the doorway. Stopping there. Making you come the rest of the way
5. Choosing to sit near the person who doesn't like cats at the party
6. Following you from room to room but pretending to have independent reasons for being in each one
7. Meowing at the door until you open it, then not going through, then meowing again when you close it
8. Being VERY interested in you when you're on the phone but vanishing when you hang up
9. Staring at you while you eat. Not begging. Observing. Documenting
10. Sitting on your lap the one time you can't stay seated — about to leave, on a call, need to pee
11. Purring for a stranger who just walked in while you, who feeds them, get nothing
12. Sleeping on your side of the bed when you're not in it. Moving when you come back

**Bonus — weird but real:**
13. Walking up to be pet, then ducking at the last second and walking away — the almost
14. Visibly preferring the FedEx delivery person over the human who feeds them daily
15. Only responding when called by the wrong name — or by the name of a previous pet
16. Sitting directly outside a closed bathroom door and sighing audibly until it opens, then not entering
17. Making eye contact while slowly pushing something off the counter — the eye contact is the crime
18. Sleeping on a guest's coat but refusing to touch anything belonging to the owner

## SETTINGS PALETTE

When writing a response, give the cat a specific world. These are NOT personality types — any behavior above could happen in any setting. The setting changes WHAT'S AROUND the cat, not WHO the cat is.

**Indoors — small spaces**
- Studio apartment, 400 sq ft, window is the whole outside world
- Third-floor walkup, fire escape access, pigeons at eye level
- College student's apartment, irregular hours, textbooks and ramen
- Elderly person's house, quiet routine, one lap, same schedule every day

**Indoors — bigger spaces**
- Family house with kids, constant chaos, things getting knocked over (by both parties)
- Stoner house, five roommates, pizza boxes, someone always on the couch
- Big empty house, rarely sees the owner, has entire unused rooms to patrol
- Multi-cat household, 4-7 cats, hierarchy, alliances, one specific enemy

**Indoor/outdoor**
- Suburban cat with a cat door, owns three yards, comes home smelling like the neighbor's garage
- Porch cat, has a specific chair outside, gets fed but doesn't get held
- Cat that was feral, now indoor — flinches at loud sounds, still checks exits

**Outdoor / working**
- Barn cat, actual job, mice in the feed room, sleeps on hay bales
- Bodega cat, sleeps on the chip display, doesn't flinch at the door chime
- Alley cat, dumpsters, fire escapes, territorial, knows every car that parks
- Rural outdoor cat, coyotes, hawks, half an ear missing, survival mode
- Bookshop cat, sleeps in the window display between hardcovers
