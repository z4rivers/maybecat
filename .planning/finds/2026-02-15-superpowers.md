# Superpower Wishes: Raw Language Ingredients
## Foraging Date: 2026-02-15
## Aisle: "What superpower would you want and why" discussions

---

**"If you choose invisibility, you are a perv and/or a thief. I choose invisibility."**
Source: ifanboy.com forum commenter
Hook: The confession hidden inside the joke — honesty dressed as self-deprecation

---

**"Everyone would pick invisibility, they're just not being honest with you."**
Source: This American Life, John Hodgman (paraphrased across multiple sources)
Hook: The implication that every "I'd fly" answer is a performance of who we want to be, not who we are

---

**"No matter which power people choose, they never use it to fight crime."**
Source: This American Life, Episode 178 "Superpowers"
Hook: The gap between what we say our values are and what we'd actually do with unlimited power

---

**"It would take the spontaneity out of life... you won't have that key human aspiration working for you — hope."**
Source: ResetEra forum, on knowing the future
Hook: Hope requires not knowing. Certainty kills the thing that keeps us going.

---

**"Mind Control/Reading essentially makes it impossible for you to have an ethical relationship with anyone."**
Source: ResetEra forum thread on superpowers as curses
Hook: The power to know everything about someone is the power to never truly love them

---

**"Any superpower is a curse if it means you have to help other people."**
Source: ResetEra forum commenter
Hook: Dark, punchy, accidentally reveals how exhausting empathy is

---

**"My kingdom for a pen, a notebook, and the hand strength to use them."**
Source: Blake Watson, SMA News Today (person with spinal muscular atrophy)
Hook: The most devastating superpower wish is one most people wouldn't even recognize as a wish

---

**"Words are powerful, but SMA does tend to limit our tools for using them."**
Source: Halsey Blocher, SMA News Today
Hook: The gap between having something to say and being able to say it

---

**"I want to be a princess. With this power, I would change other princesses to cats so I could be the only princess."**
Source: Evis, age 7, Colombia (Children International)
Hook: Already a complete power move. Pure dominance energy from a 7-year-old.

---

**"To fly. I don't know why. To get home?!?"**
Source: Daniela, age 4, Mexico (Children International)
Hook: The most honest answer anyone has ever given. Every adult answer is just a longer version of this.

---

**"I'd pause time just to take longer naps."**
Source: wikireplies.com compilation
Hook: The saddest superpower is the one that's just asking for rest

---

**"Rewind and fix every awkward thing I've ever said."**
Source: wikireplies.com compilation
Hook: Time travel as shame management — the wish is the wound

---

**"My clone can deal with awkward conversations."**
Source: wikireplies.com compilation
Hook: Outsourcing your own humanity because the original can't handle it

---

**"I could rob a bank and vanish. Just kidding... unless?"**
Source: wikireplies.com compilation
Hook: The ellipsis is doing all the work. Every "just kidding" is a confession.

---

**"Jump between realities just to avoid responsibilities."**
Source: wikireplies.com compilation
Hook: Multiverse travel reframed as the ultimate avoidance strategy

---

**"I want the power to quit my job and just start doing what I want in life."**
Source: betterresponses.com compilation
Hook: That's not a superpower. That's just money. The wish reveals what they think is impossible.

---

**FOUND WHOLE: "Hack the simulation and figure out if we're real."**
Source: wikireplies.com compilation
Hook: Existential crisis in eight words. Works as oblique answer to almost any question.
Cat energy: Gonzo (weird and proud of it) meets Steven Wright (deadpan surreal)

---

**"You. Don't. Want. To. Read. Minds. It's just an awful ability."**
Source: Jay Raph, Medium article on mind reading as terrible superpower
Hook: The rhythm of the punctuation. Each period is a door slamming shut.

---

**"Knowing every single thought your partner has had about you would be enough to drive anyone insane."**
Source: Medium, "Why Being a Mind Reader is Actually a Terrible Superpower"
Hook: Love requires not knowing. The wish for total knowledge is the wish for the end of love.

---

**"The ability to leave the house with plenty of time to get where I'm going, instead of flying out the door already 10 minutes late."**
Source: Alison Chambers, The Poke (mundane superpowers thread)
Hook: The saddest superpower is just being on time. Adults don't dream of flying — they dream of not being late.

---

**FOUND WHOLE: "It's the power of the perfectionist, the nostalgic, and the over-achiever."**
Source: eathealthy365.com, on why people choose time manipulation
Hook: Three wounds in one sentence. Works as answer to "why do you do this to yourself."
Cat energy: Dorothy Parker (acerbic elegance)

---

**"Intelligence goes hand-in-hand with loneliness. It doesn't allow you to ignore the indifference of physics."**
Source: Superpower Wiki, quoting Najimi Ajimu (Medaka Box)
Hook: Knowing everything is the loneliest thing you can do

---

**"Teleportation shows you want to bypass the obstacles and get right to the good part."**
Source: eathealthy365.com
Hook: Every shortcut is a confession about what you can't endure

---

**"Kim Siever: Invisibility. Then I can find what people are saying about me behind my back."**
Source: jeffmilner.com comments
Hook: The superpower wish that's just anxiety with a cape

**"Any benefits you'd get from invisibility would get old and make you feel guilty."**
Source: ifanboy.com discussion
