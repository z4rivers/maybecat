# Foraging: Product Reviews - Emotional Extremes
**Date:** 2026-02-15
**Aisle:** Amazon reviews, Yelp, Google Maps - the rage, the devotion, the disproportionate drama

---

**"Every time I see these useless bags hanging, I smile and feel stupid."**
Source: Amazon / hanging storage bags
Hook: The resigned self-awareness. Smiling at your own bad decisions is a whole mood.

---

**"This is one of 5-10 purchases I made in my life that made me feel dumb."**
Source: Amazon / supplement product
Hook: The specificity of "5-10" -- they've ranked their regrets.

---

**"I waited about a year or more to make sure they work, and that's another waste of my life."**
Source: Amazon / supplement product
Hook: The patience of disappointment. They gave it a YEAR and then counted that as a separate loss.

---

**"Not as big as I thought but I'm female and have been lied to all my life about the size of stuff."**
Source: Amazon / unnamed product
Hook: One sentence, three layers of meaning. The construction does ALL the work.

---

**"Great for waterproof kindling, crap for murder."**

---

**"They sit there mocking me as I drunkenly cradle myself to sleep, cold and alone in my pathetic excuse of an apartment. Great product though 10/10."**
Source: Amazon / bulk condoms

**"No longer consumed by seething anger and animosity towards thick-skinned yellow fruit, I was able to concentrate on my love of theatre."**

Hook: "Seething anger towards thick-skinned yellow fruit" as a real backstory. The pivot to theatre.

---

**"Our marriage has never been healthier, AND we've even incorporated it into our lovemaking."**
Source: Amazon / banana slicer
Hook: A banana slicer saving a marriage AND entering the bedroom. The casual escalation.

---

**"Do you want your hair to be greasier, dirtier, more unmanageable, and smell like you washed your hair with grandma's perfume? Then this is the dry shampoo for you."**
Source: Amazon / dry shampoo

---

**"My parents looking at me through the other side of the glass disappointed in me as they walked away."**

---

**"It is day 87 and the horses have accepted me as one of their own. I have grown to understand and respect their gentle ways."**

**"I know that those who sent me will not relent. They will send others in my place. But we will be ready."**
Source: Amazon / horse head mask (same review)

---

**"I love them so much I couldn't even bring myself to use them. I drew faces on them, they are now my friends and I have a little over 40 sponges."**

**"There is too much water."**
