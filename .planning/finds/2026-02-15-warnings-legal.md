# Foraging: Official/Legal/Warning Language
**Date:** 2026-02-15
**Aisle:** The absurdity of official, legal, and warning language

---

**"Go soothingly on the grease mud, as there lurks the skid demon."**
Source: 1962 Honda motorcycle owner's manual, translated safety rules
Hook: The formality + "skid demon" is accidentally mythological.

---

**"Give big space to the festive dog that makes sport in roadway."**
Source: 1962 Honda motorcycle owner's manual, translated safety rules
Hook: "Festive dog" is the most generous description of a stray dog ever written.

---

**"When a passenger of the foot hooves in sight, tootel the horn trumpet melodiously at first. If he still obstacles your passage, tootel him with vigor."**
Source: 1962 Honda motorcycle owner's manual, translated safety rules
Hook: "Tootel him with vigor" is a perfect escalation instruction for anything.

---

**"Do not explode the exhaust box at him."**
Source: 1962 Honda motorcycle owner's manual, translated safety rules
Hook: Removed from context, this is a life philosophy.

---

**"This restriction will not apply in the event of a widespread viral infection transmitted via bites or contact with bodily fluids that causes human corpses to reanimate and seek to consume living human flesh, blood, brain or nerve tissue and is likely to result in the fall of organized civilization."**
Source: Amazon Web Services Terms of Service, Section 57.10 (Lumberyard game engine)
Hook: Real, actual legal language. The phrase "the fall of organized civilization" in a TOS.

---

**"Wearing of this garment does not enable you to fly."**
Source: Warning label on a child-sized Superman costume
Hook: The neutral legal tone acknowledging someone expected flight.

---

**"Warning: This product moves when used."**
Source: Warning label on a Razor scooter
Hook: The product does exactly one thing and they had to warn you about it.

---

**"Do not look into laser with remaining eye."**
Source: Warning label on a laser pointer
Hook: "Remaining" does a LOT of heavy lifting. Implies you already lost one.

---

**"Danger: Avoid Death."**
Source: Warning label on a small tractor (Wacky Warning Label contest winner)
Hook: The most efficient advice ever given. Two words. Universal.

---

**"Because his brain was sitting on my desk in a jar." / "Yes, it is possible that he could have been alive and practicing law."**
Source: Court transcript — doctor questioned about autopsy procedure
Hook: The witness destroyed an entire profession in a subordinate clause.

---

**"How was your first marriage terminated?" "By death." "And by whose death was it terminated?" "Take a guess."**
Source: Court transcript — lawyer questioning a witness
Hook: "Take a guess" with a straight face, under oath.

---

**"What is your date of birth?" "July 15th." "What year?" "Every year."**
Source: Court transcript — lawyer-witness exchange
Hook: Technically the most correct answer ever given in a courtroom.

---

**"What gear were you in at the moment of the impact?" "Gucci sweats and Reeboks."**
Source: Court transcript — lawyer-witness exchange
Hook: The answer to a question nobody asked, and the perfect answer to the one they did.

---

**"Such news of an amicable settlement having made this court happier than a tick on a fat dog because it is otherwise busier than a one-legged cat in a sandbox and, quite frankly, would have rather jumped naked off of a twelve-foot step ladder into a five-gallon bucket of porcupines than have presided over a two-week trial."**
Source: Judge Martin Sheehan, 16th Kentucky Circuit Court, official judicial opinion
Hook: A real judge put "bucket of porcupines" in a legal document.

---

**"...a trial which, no doubt, would have made the jury more confused than a hungry baby in a topless bar and made the parties and their attorneys madder than mosquitoes in a mannequin factory."**
Source: Same Judge Sheehan opinion (continued)
Hook: "Mosquitoes in a mannequin factory" is an image that gets worse the longer you think about it.

---

**"My walkie talkie is busted so I had to send this note."**
Source: Netflix's cease-and-desist letter to a Stranger Things-themed pop-up bar
Hook: Opening line of a real legal letter. The formality of "cease and desist" vs. walkie talkie.

---

**"So please don't make us call your mom."**
Source: Netflix cease-and-desist letter (closing line)
Hook: A corporate legal threat that ends with a mom joke.

---

**"This is the part where I'm supposed to cite the United States Copyright Act and threaten you with eternal damnation for not complying. But you already earned that with those Garfield movies."**
Source: Doobie Brothers' cease-and-desist letter to Bill Murray
Hook: A real lawyer dragged Garfield in a legal document.

---

**"It seems like the only person who uses our clients' music without permission more than you do is Donald Trump."**
Source: Doobie Brothers' cease-and-desist letter to Bill Murray
Hook: Comparative infringement as a burn.

---

**"We'd almost be OK with it if the shirts weren't so damn ugly."**
Source: Doobie Brothers' cease-and-desist letter to Bill Murray
Hook: Aesthetic criticism as a legal argument.

---

**"This email may contain information that is confidential, privileged, or unsuitable for overly sensitive persons with low self-esteem, no sense of humour, or irrational religious beliefs."**
Source: Humorous corporate email disclaimer (widely circulated)
Hook: A disclaimer disclaiming the reader themselves.

---

**"Not responsible for loss of composure, illusions, virginity, or any other artifact or item tangible or otherwise."**
Source: Spoof bar/venue disclaimer
Hook: "Loss of illusions" buried casually between composure and virginity.

---

**"We thought that we would never see / A suit to compensate a tree."**
Source: Judge J.H. Gillis, Fisher v. Lowe (Michigan Court of Appeals), wrote entire opinion as a poem
Hook: A real legal ruling delivered in rhyming couplets.

---

**"Keep it away from incompetent people, such as children or pregnant women."**
Source: Translated Chinese laser engraver manual
Hook: The "such as" is doing violence.

---

**"Do Not Use Beyond Usage."**
Source: Badly translated product instruction manual
Hook: Accidentally the most profound instruction ever written.
