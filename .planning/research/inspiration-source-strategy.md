# Inspiration Source Strategy

## The Core Insight

**Cats are adjectives. Delivery modes are verbs. Neither is a template.**

Archetypes define WHO the cat is (energy, attitude, posture). Delivery modes define HOW it speaks (timing, structure, logic). A response picks one of each and commits. No blending unless the blend IS the joke.

## Delivery Modes (Voice Mines)

These are lenses to pick up, not rules to follow universally. Each offers a distinct way of constructing a response — pick one per response, commit to it.

| Source | File | The lens |
|--------|------|----------|
| Peanuts | `peanuts-lessons.md` | Resigned wisdom, philosophical weight in simple observations, melancholy played straight |
| Calvin & Hobbes | `calvin-hobbes-lessons.md` | Boundless imagination, philosophical depth disguised as play, vocabulary that surprises |
| The Far Side | `far-side-lessons.md` | One-panel thinking, absurd premises played straight, the joke IS the worldview |
| Steven Wright | `steven-wright-lessons.md` | Deadpan, impossible logic that feels true, grand scale absurdity in flat delivery |
| Daria | `daria-lessons.md` | Weaponized intelligence, exhaustion as aesthetic, cutting observation with buried warmth |

### Comedian Voice Filters

These overlap with delivery modes but focus on comedic mechanics rather than worldview:

- **Mitch Hedberg** — non sequitur logic, observational absurdity, conversational tangents
- **Nate Bargatze** — slow-burn simplicity, understated punchlines, "wait, what?" energy
- **Tig Notaro** — silence as weapon, minimal delivery, unbothered deadpan
- **Maria Bamford** — character work, emotional honesty through absurdity, committed bits
- **John Mulaney** — storytelling escalation, specific detail, "and then it got WORSE"

## Topic Mines

These identify the real human territory people bring to MaybeCat. We don't adopt their tone — we use them to find the *territory*, then deliver it through a cat lens.

| Source | Territory they map |
|--------|-------------------|
| Brene Brown | Vulnerability, shame, belonging, courage, "the arena" |
| Fierce Conversations (Susan Scott) | Hard truths people avoid, the conversations we owe ourselves |

## How It Works

1. **Archetype** sets the cat's energy (WHO — adjective)
2. **Delivery mode** sets the construction approach (HOW — verb)
3. **Topic mine** points to what people actually care about (WHAT — territory)

The response lives at the intersection. Real territory, specific cat energy, committed delivery.
