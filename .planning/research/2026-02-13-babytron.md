# BabyTron Research — Raw Material for MaybeCat

## WHO HE IS

**James Johnson III** aka BabyTron. Born 2000, Ypsilanti, Michigan. Part of **ShittyBoyz** (with StanWill and TrDee). XXL 2022 Freshman. Featured on Eminem's "Tobey" (2024) with Big Sean. Dad was a rapper and friends with Violent J from ICP. Graduated Lincoln High School June 2019, dropped Bin Reaper three months later.

"Shitty in Detroit means good — like crispy clothes is shitty. Having money is shitty." The name started as BabyTron's ad-lib, became the crew name when a cameraman shot their first real video.

---

## THE BABYTRON ENERGY

The core of BabyTron for MaybeCat purposes:
- **Deadpan delivery of absolutely wild statements** — says outrageous things like they're weather reports
- **Punchline-per-line density** — reviewer said "It's like a punchline every single line"
- **References from EVERYWHERE** — NBA deep cuts, anime, video games, Harry Potter, Star Wars, scam culture, food
- **"Uniquely swagless"** — described as "Fortnite-core (Bape hoodies, Just Do It T-shirts), goofy haircut, looks like anyone who spends too much time on the internet"
- **The confidence of someone who doesn't need you to laugh**
- **Competing with himself** — Tom Breihan (Stereogum): "clearly competing with himself, constantly trying to come up with the wildest things he can say"

## THE BABYTRON STRUCTURE — How His Bars Work

### The "Feel Like" Formula
BabyTron's signature move: "Feel like [unexpected reference]" — takes an athlete, character, or cultural figure and maps one specific trait onto his current situation. The comparison is always sideways, never obvious.

### The Setup-Twist
Line 1 sets up what sounds like a normal brag. Line 2 lands the comparison from a completely unexpected world (NBA benchwarmer, PlayStation character, chess piece, Frankenstein).

### The Deadpan Wild Claim
States something absolutely outrageous as if it's a weather report. No emphasis, no wink. The humor is in the delivery gap — the words are insane but the tone says "obviously."

### Multi-Reference Stacking
"Maybe it's four things that all go together in one line" — he layers references so one bar contains a sports reference, a scam reference, and a punchline simultaneously.

### The Scam Vocabulary as Metaphor
Credit card terminology, VPNs, dark web, Bitcoin, Western Union — all used as metaphors for regular life situations. "Plugged in" means both connected and scamming.

## CULTURAL CONTEXT

### Scam Rap
- Genre pioneered by **Teejayx6** — lyrics that literally sound like tutorials for credit card fraud
- BabyTron's version is more tongue-in-cheek — uses scam vocabulary as a vehicle for wordplay rather than literal how-to
- Michigan Daily described the sound as "bouncy beats filled with high synths and drum hits that produce a sound similar to a loading screen on a low-budget video game"
- The flow: "offhanded, almost apathetic" — "often cutting themselves off in the middle of a bar, failing to rhyme consecutive lines or rapping completely off-beat"
Source: https://www.michigandaily.com/arts/teejayx6-shittyboyz-and-rise-scam-rap/

### Detroit/Michigan Scene
- BabyTron grew up listening to Doughboyz Cashout, FMB Deezy, Team Eastside, Bandgang
- ShittyBoyz released six albums in under four years
- The scene: Ice Wear Vezzo, 42 Dugg, Big Sean, Babyface Ray, Tee Grizzley
- **Buffs** (Cartier Buffalo horn glasses) = Detroit status symbol, shows up constantly in lyrics

### The "Swagless" Aesthetic
- "Fortnite-core" — Bape hoodies, Just Do It T-shirts
- Goofy haircut, looks like a regular internet kid
- The OPPOSITE of traditional rap flexing — anti-swag IS the swag
Source: https://www.michigandaily.com/arts/teejayx6-shittyboyz-and-rise-scam-rap/

### Beat Selection as Identity
- Raps over Harry Potter theme, Star Wars theme, Darth Vader theme, Radiohead, 80s synth pop, video game loading screens
- "Prince of the Mitten" = 19 consecutive different beats
- "Emperor of the Universe" = 21 consecutive beats (directed by Cole Bennett/Lyrical Lemonade)
- "Lord of the Multiverse" = 45 beats spanning every year in hip-hop history (11 minutes)
- "100 Bars" = conceptual video where he appears as 100 different suspects, one bar each

### The Eminem Connection
Featured on "Tobey" (July 2024) from The Death of Slim Shady — with Big Sean and Eminem. BabyTron takes the first verse and chorus. Three Detroit rappers on one track.
Source: https://variety.com/2024/music/news/eminem-tobey-big-sean-and-babytron-new-single-1236058822/

## MAYBECAT RELOCATION POTENTIAL

### What translates:
1. **The deadpan wild claim** — "Feel like Clyde Drexler with the slides" energy as a cat answering void questions
2. **Unexpected reference worlds** — a cat pulling from NBA, anime, video games, scam culture
3. **The anti-swag confidence** — not trying to impress, just stating facts that happen to be insane
4. **Punchline density** — one-line hits, no setup needed, every word works
5. **The "I wouldn't scam your mother, probably not" energy** — hedged honesty, the "probably" does all the work
6. **Scam vocabulary as life metaphor** — "you done sold the bag" applies to ANYONE'S 2 AM question
7. **The specificity** — not "a basketball player" but "Frank Kaminsky." Not "a video game" but "Ratchet & Clank." Specificity = comedy.

### What to watch for:
- Scam-specific references might not land for general audience without context
- NBA deep cuts (Aquille Carr, Seventh Woods) are hilarious if you know, invisible if you don't
- The energy is FAST — MaybeCat needs to slow it to one-line delivery
- Detroit slang needs translation sometimes ("buffs" = Cartier glasses, "shitty" = good)

### The BabyTron voice for a cat:
A cat who states the most outrageous observations about your life as if they're obvious facts. Pulls references from worlds you didn't expect a cat to know about. Never explains the joke. The confidence of someone who freestyle-recorded an entire album and it was all bangers. Says "probably" and "might" in ways that make the statement MORE certain, not less.
