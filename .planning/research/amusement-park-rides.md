## 2. THE REGRET MOMENT (Restraints Click, Too Late Now)

### The Lift Hill Psychology

- Vertical lift hills are described as terrifying because "it's slow and all you see is sky"
- Riders describe "a feeling that you could just freefall backward as the coaster is slowly ratcheting upward"
- The anti-rollback dog creates the iconic clack-clack-clack sound ascending the lift hill -- it's literally a safety device but it sounds like a countdown to doom
- One rider: "If a lift were slow at extreme heights, I wouldn't know if I could handle being up that high for that long"

### Restraint Horror Stories

- A teenager was holding his lap bar up in the air when the train returned -- the restraint had come loose from the housing
- Rider experienced an over-the-shoulder restraint (OTSR) opening at the top of a lift on Medusa and held it down for the entire ride
- On Raging Bull, lap bars flew open in the station before the ride left
- A PTC lap bar was observed popping open on Thunder Run despite being checked and fully lowered
- A rider's son experienced an unlocked lap bar on Magnum for the entire duration -- only the seatbelt kept him in

Sources: [UltimateRollerCoaster - Restraint Fail](https://www.ultimaterollercoaster.com/forums/roller-coasters-theme-parks/227481), [CoasterForce - Best/Worst Restraints](https://coasterforce.com/forums/threads/best-and-worst-restraint.42317/)

## 7. STUCK ON RIDES (Breakdown Horror Stories)

### The It's a Small World Torture

- 2022: Disney World's ride broke down and dozens of people were stuck listening to animatronic characters sing over and over -- "it was like torture"
- 2009: A guest with quadriplegia got stuck in the "Goodbye Room" for 30-40 minutes with "blaring Christmas carols" aggravating medical conditions. He sued Disney. A jury awarded him $8,000.
- One person described being stuck in the "Holland" section with dolls singing that song "over and over and over"
- Theme Park Insider called it: "Getting stuck on It's a Small World when they forget to turn off the soundtrack" -- among the most painful experiences

### Upside Down for Hours

**Wisconsin Fireball (2023):**
- Eight passengers trapped upside down for over THREE HOURS after a mechanical failure at the Forest County Festival
- First passenger reached ground 2 hours after emergency dispatch; last at 3+ hours
- A girl told rescuers to unstrap an older man first because "he was visibly struggling and appeared to have passed out"
- 50 people from three counties helped with rescue

**Portland, Oregon:**
- Nearly two dozen riders stuck upside down for 30+ minutes
- "What was supposed to be a quick second of being upside down" lasted half an hour

**Six Flags Great America (1998):**
- The Demon riders stranded upside down for two hours; four hospitalized

### Other Breakdown Nightmares

- **Space Mountain with lights on:** Someone got stuck when the lights came on and had to walk down "unstable-looking staircases from a hill" -- seeing the mundane reality behind the magic
- **The Mummy fire scene:** Stuck in the final scene with fire going across the ceiling for about 20 minutes. "The heat became unbearable with people screaming to get out"
- **Millennium Force, Cedar Point:** First 3 cars jolted to a halt and riders stared straight at the sun for about an hour
- **Skyliner gondolas:** Passengers stuck for up to three hours, rescued by firefighters with ladder trucks
- **300-foot tower swing, Knott's Berry Farm:** Riders stranded in mid-air for nearly four hours (2012)
- **Lightning strike at Six Flags Great Adventure (2004):** Knocked out power, stranded riders upside down 75 feet in the air

### The Dinosaur Ride (Animal Kingdom)

- One parent and young daughter: ride broke down "at the scariest part" -- very dark with sounds of dinosaurs, then went completely dark, the coaster swayed, and it was terrifying

Sources: [NPR - Wisconsin Fireball](https://www.npr.org/2023/07/05/1186003765/roller-coaster-upside-down-wisconsin-fireball), [CNN - Crandon Wisconsin](https://www.cnn.com/2023/07/04/us/wisconsin-roller-coaster-riders-stuck/index.html), [Gawker - Small World Nightmare](https://www.gawker.com/5992554/nightmare-scenario-comes-true-for-disabled-man-stuck-on-broken-its-a-small-world-ride-with-infamous-theme-song-blaring-on-a-continuous-loop), [Ranker - Worst Rides to Get Stuck On](https://www.ranker.com/list/worst-amusement-park-rides-to-be-stuck-on/ashley-reign)

## 9. ENTHUSIAST JARGON (The Insider Vocabulary)

### Positive Sensations
- **Ejector airtime:** Violent, sudden negative g-forces that would fling you from the seat without restraints
- **Floater airtime:** Gentle weightlessness, near-perfect floating sensation
- **Zero-g:** Moments of complete weightlessness from speed and forces
- **Airtime hills:** The hills designed to produce that stomach-lifting sensation

### Negative Experiences
- **Stapling:** When a ride operator ratchets your lap bar down too tight, killing the airtime sensation
- **Head banging:** Your head getting slammed side to side, particularly on OTSRs (over-the-shoulder restraints)
- **Rattle:** Vibration/roughness in the ride, increasingly criticized on new coasters
- **Trim brakes:** Mid-course brakes that reduce speed (enthusiasts HATE these -- they neuter the ride)
- **Grey-out:** Partial vision loss during high positive g-forces (precursor to blackout)

### Community Slang
- **Cred:** A roller coaster on your count ("I got 47 new creds this season")
- **Coaster count:** Total unique coasters ridden -- THE status metric
- **Cred whore:** Someone who will ride anything to increase their count -- "will go to extreme lengths to obtain the cred"
- **Home park:** Your closest or most-visited park, "which may or may not invoke fanboy-ism"
- **+1:** A minor coaster ridden just for the cred count
- **Cloning:** When a manufacturer builds the same ride at multiple parks

### Ride Elements
- **Batwing:** Teardrop-shaped vertical loop
- **Cobra roll:** Element shaped like an elongated cobra
- **Barrel roll / heartline roll:** Track twists 360 degrees around the rider's chest level
- **Corkscrew:** 360-degree rotation perpendicular to track direction
- **Stall:** Inverted hang time where the train essentially pauses upside down

Sources: [CoasterForce Glossary](https://coasterforce.com/glossary/), [Cupcakes and Coasters A-Z Guide](https://www.cupcakesandcoasters.com/2019/05/coaster-enthusiast-dictionary.html), [UltimateRollerCoaster Glossary](https://www.ultimaterollercoaster.com/coasters/glossary/)

## 10. RIDES THAT LOOK HARMLESS (Then Destroy You)

### The Deception Pattern

- **Phoenix at Knoebels:** "Didn't look like much more than a basic out and back wooden coaster" -- then insane airtime
- **Hades 360:** "Might seem like a regular old wooden roller coaster, but looks are deceiving" -- hits 70 mph, first wooden coaster with a 360-degree roll, world's longest underground tunnel
- **SkyCycle (Japan):** Appears like "a gentle commute" -- actually a track 50 feet above ground where you PEDAL forward like a bicycle, "all that's keeping you from dropping to your deaths is a seatbelt"
- **Revenge of the Mummy:** Hidden backwards launch that "completely shocks first-timers"

### The "It Looks Safe" Energy (Perfect for MaybeCat)

These descriptions all follow the pattern: It LOOKS like one thing. It IS another. This is advice gold:
- "It looked gentle from the ground."
- "At first glance, it didn't look like much."
- "Looks are deceiving."
