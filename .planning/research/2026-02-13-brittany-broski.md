# Brittany Broski — Raw Material Research

## WHO SHE IS

Brittany Alexis Tomlinson (b. May 10, 1997, Georgia; raised in Dallas, Texas). Content creator, comedian, podcaster, singer. Went viral in August 2019 with a TikTok of her trying kombucha for the first time — rapidly changing facial expressions that became a universal reaction meme. Got fired from her bank job afterward. Now: 7.6M TikTok followers, 4.6M across YouTube/Instagram. TIME100 Creators 2025. Hosts The Broski Report podcast and Royal Court (medieval-themed YouTube talk show). Debut single "The Sun" (April 2025) via Atlantic Music Group. Texas A&M graduate (Communications, magna cum laude, 2018). Self-described "theater kid at heart."

---

## THE BROSKI ENERGY (What Makes Her Relocatable)

- **DRAMATIC about EVERYTHING.** A sandwich, a fragrance, a celebrity sighting — all treated with the gravity of a life-altering event.
- **Elaborate vocabulary applied to mundane things.** Uses "aggravated mental illness" as a medical diagnosis for what she does. Calls herself a "bearded woman" at a circus.
- **Cultural omnivore.** References range from Game of Thrones to flamenco music to Chris Stapleton to Wattpad fan fiction to the evolution of fire engines from horse-drawn carriages.
- **Performative outrage that's actually genuine.** The drama is real. The vocabulary is real. She just happens to be THAT person about everything.
- **Self-aware about being dramatic but DOES NOT STOP.** Knows she's unhinged. Does not intend to fix it.
- **Southern roots + internet brain.** Texas country girl meets Tumblr-rotted millennial. The accent slips in and out.
- **Fangirl energy turned professional.** Obsessions (Hozier, Harry Styles, Sebastian Stan) are the engine of her content, not a side note.
- **The "jump off the cliff and hope you know how to fly" person.** Everything is a leap. Everything is the most important thing that's ever happened.

## THE BROSKI VOCABULARY (Recurring Patterns)

- Uses medical/clinical language for emotional states ("aggravated mental illness," "intensive therapy")
- Uses grand-scale metaphors for personal experiences ("war of the worlds inside her mind," "fan fiction," "ripped the towel open")
- Mixes highbrow references with lowbrow delivery (Game of Thrones + "check this shit out")
- Southern idioms that slip through the LA polish
- Treats every obsession as THE obsession — not a casual interest, a life event
- Self-deprecation that's actually self-celebration in disguise
- Physical comedy descriptions ("a little trickle of piss ran down my leg," "I blacked out")
- ALL CAPS energy even in print — you can HEAR her escalating
