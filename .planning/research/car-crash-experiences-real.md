# Car Crash Experiences — Real People's Language

Compiled from Reddit, Quora, NPR, Medium essays, insurance claims, first responder accounts, psychology research. What it FELT like to be inside the wreck.

## TIME DISTORTION
- "Everything seemed to go into slow motion. There was a very long gap between the sound of impact and the beginning of the car's spin."
- "The other cars on the motorway seemed to be moving extremely slowly, almost as if they were stationary."
- "I was surprised by how clear and vivid everything became, and how much detail I was taking in. There was a strange sense of quietness too."
- "Time slowed down. I was very calm and waited for the right moment. Everything seemed to happen in slow motion. The event happened perhaps in a second or two, but it felt much longer." — neuroscientist Marc Wittmann on his own skid
- "I watched the crash in silent slow motion until I 'came to' in the front seat."
- 71% of people recall experiencing altered passage of time during accidents

## THE GLASS
- "I saw the car's windscreen shattering. The glass sprayed out so slowly, like a fan, and it looked beautiful. All the pieces were shining in the sun."
- "I felt like I was floating through the air, almost as if I wasn't going to come down. I looked into the sun and it was like being on a plane, when you're above the clouds and it's a brilliant white colour."

## THE WEIRD CALM / PEACE
- Dax Shepard: "I definitely had this moment where I was like, 'Oh wow, I'm going to die in a second.' And the feeling was shockingly comforting."
- "I felt oddly at peace." — Reddit user who bled out and was resuscitated
- "Rather than panic, the author found a peculiar sense of peace nestled in the shattering glass and contorted metal."
- "A strange, peaceful calm, knowing I'd lived a decent life."

## MENTAL SILENCE
- "My mind descended into a welcoming void of thoughts." — first mental silence the person had ever experienced
- "It didn't just damage my car, it changed how I moved through the world."

## THE MUSIC STILL PLAYING
- Entire Steve Hoffman forum thread of people sharing what song was playing during their crash
- "I arrived on scene first and the radio was going in the Honda, with a kids' CD playing." — first responder
- After flipping his car, a driver turned UP the volume on AC/DC's "Highway to Hell" and waited for help

## THE AIRBAG
- Hits at one-twentieth of a second. 160+ decibels — louder than a gunshot
- Releases sodium hydroxide, chemical dust. Smells like burnt rubber
- "An explosion that caused hearing to be very muffled with ringing in the ear"

## DISSOCIATION — WATCHING YOURSELF
- "She watched herself force open the door and look around."
- Survivors describe "feeling detached, as if watching themselves in a movie"
- "Before, driving was automatic. Afterward, it became a place where my body reacted before I had time to think."

## "AM I DEAD?"
- Dax Shepard: "For the next three days, I'd just be places and I felt very peculiar like, 'Am I dead?'"
- "Now things, people and places etc are not how I remember or know them to be."
- Maps to derealization/depersonalization — can last days to years

## HANDS INTERTWINED
- "When both regained consciousness after the crash, their hands were already intertwined."
- "I should've died. Instead, something unseen held my hand."

## LIFE FLASHING
- "It felt as if I was watching a movie of my life. Everyone I have ever loved, childhood memories, all of it, cycled through my mind as if saying goodbye to me."

## THE BODY / ADRENALINE
- "Bodies launched across the freeway like slung dolls"
- Adrenaline deception: walk away thinking "that wasn't too bad" — hours later the full extent reveals
- "Your hands, legs, or entire body tremble"
- One survivor: Having the "color of life" sucked out of him

## LOSS OF SMELL / SENSORY
- "Spectator in your own life" — watching the world through a pane of glass
- Odor-triggered memories experienced as "here-and-now" — brief dissociative flashbacks from gasoline smell

## ACCIDENTALLY POETIC INSURANCE CLAIMS

- "I bumped into a lamp-post which was obscured by human beings."
- "The accident was caused by me waving to the man I hit last week."
- "Leaving home for work I drove out of my drive and straight into a bus; the bus was five minutes early."
- "I had one eye on the parked car, another on an approaching truck, and another on the woman behind."
- "A lamp-post bumped into my car, damaging it in two places."
- "In an attempt to kill a fly I drove into a telephone pole."
- "I started to turn and it was at this point I noticed a camel and an elephant tethered at the verge."
- "I was driving along when I saw two kangaroos copulating in the middle of the road causing me to ejaculate through the sun roof."
