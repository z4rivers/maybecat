## WHO SHE IS

TiaCorine (born Tia Corine Thompson-Shultz), rapper/singer from Winston-Salem, North Carolina. Self-described "anime trap" pioneer. Mom, Bojangles alumna, tattoo collector, anime obsessive, Kill Bill devotee. Black, Japanese, and Shoshone (Native American). XXL Freshman 2023. Signed to South Coast Music Group (same label as DaBaby, Toosii). Released trilogy: *34Corine* EP (2020), *I Can't Wait* (2022), *Almost There* EP (2024), *CORINIAN* (2025).

---

## THE TIA ENERGY (for MaybeCat context)

The core of Tia's energy: does what she wants, doesn't explain it, treats her whole life like a cartoon, and will tell you to shut up in such a cute way you don't realize she told you to shut up. Food-adjacent (literally got her first brand deal at Bojangles, where she used to be a shift manager). Weird and proud. A cat who got famous by accident and refused to change.

## SONG / AESTHETIC ENERGY NOTES

These aren't direct quotes but describe the sonic and visual worlds that are distinctly Tia:

- **"FreakyT"** — Unapologetic confidence anthem. The alter-ego "Freaky T" as badge of honor. Viral across TikTok. Remix with Latto.
- **"Lotto"** — "Scratch off like lotto / Bitch try ya luck" — the line that went viral three separate times on TikTok. Born from a shower thought.
- **"ATE"** — Bass-blasted, drill-infused single celebrating the fact that she's eating while rivals struggle. Title says it all.
- **"Ironic"** — Kenny Beats production. Glitchy, off-kilter, candy-colored fever dream. Mocks the idea of trying too hard — her success looks effortless, and that's the irony.
- **"CORINIAN"** — Final form. 17 tracks. Features Saweetie, Flo Milli, Smino, Wiz Khalifa, JID, Pouya. The end of the trilogy.

## MAYBECAT ENERGY MAPPING

What makes Tia's voice useful for MaybeCat relocations:

**1. "I do what I want" as default state** — Not rebellion, just baseline. The cat who never considered not being themselves.

**2. The accidental viral moment** — She didn't plan "Lotto." She didn't even like it. It just happened. Cat energy: "I wasn't trying to impress you."

**3. Cute-but-will-destroy-you duality** — "A girl chewing bubble gum, but telling you to shut up. But in such a cute way, you don't even realize she told you to shut up." This IS a cat.

**4. The cutting-ties wisdom** — "My best form of self-care is cutting people off" is a genuinely sharp MaybeCat line as-is. So is "I know it's time to cut ties when somebody triggers something in the past that made me crash out."

**5. "I just wake up and sometimes I feel like doing this"** — No plans, no apologies. Pure cat scheduling.

**6. Food-first energy** — Bojangles arc. "ATE" as a song title. The physicality of her brand.

**7. The "I'm shocked people like me" authenticity** — "I'm always shocked that these people like me. I'm just this person from Winston-Salem." Earnest without being soft.

**8. The not-fitting-in-anywhere as power** — "I never really fit in anywhere, and I still feel like that with music." This is the cat who sits between all the groups.
