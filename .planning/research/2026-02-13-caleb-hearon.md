# Caleb Hearon Research — Raw Material for MaybeCat

**Date:** 2026-02-13
**Handle:** @calebsaysthings (Twitter/X, TikTok, Instagram, Substack)
**Born:** January 24, 1995, Chillicothe, Missouri
**Background:** Grew up poor, gay, rural Missouri. Single mom. Church kid. Degree in sociopolitical communication from Missouri State. Worked as admin assistant at Chicago ad agency before comedy took off. Chicago improv (iO, Second City), then LA (2020-2023), then NYC. HBO special "Model Comedian" (Sep 2025). Variety's 10 Comics to Watch. Rolling Stone's #6 Most Influential Creator (2025). Cast in Devil Wears Prada 2.

**The Voice:** Deadpan. Understated. Observational humor that's more observation than humor. Captures universal truths in one sentence. Says enormous things in small voices. The tweet you screenshot and send to three people. Midwestern warmth underneath razor precision. Never mean-spirited but never soft.

## PHILOSOPHY / WORLDVIEW (Not direct jokes, but the engine)

- **On comedy:** "I do think the first and most important thing onstage is to be funny. Then really after that, it's just like, 'Is it true to me? Is it loving?'"
- **On art vs. venture capital:** Rejects "treating art like it's venture capital, like it needs to be grown end over end over end."
- **On success:** Doesn't want 20 million followers. Doesn't want yachts. Wants to make stuff with friends.
- **On vulnerability:** "It doesn't feel very vulnerable to me." Just telling the truth.
- **On politics in comedy:** "I would hate for the response from artists to be that we say less or do less."
- **On his influences:** His mom. The women in his family. Richard Pryor, George Carlin. "A lot of women, and especially fat women" -- Mo'Nique, Roseanne.
- **On podcasting:** "Never ever" listens to other podcasts.

## WHAT WORKS FOR MAYBECAT (Analysis)

### The Caleb Energy That Translates:
1. **Universal truths in one deadpan sentence** -- the airbnb tweet, the cigarettes tweet, the "17,000 songs" tweet
2. **Making the mundane feel enormous** -- googling recipes you'll never make, staring at your phone before bed
3. **Understated devastation** -- "most people are going through nothing"
4. **Anti-ambition as wisdom** -- "I don't really want 20 million followers or to own yachts"
5. **Self-awareness without self-deprecation** -- "fat, gay, from Missouri, not Republican yet"
6. **The deadpan escalation** -- "You're so soft. I'll kill you to death with a gun."

### Lines That Could Relocate Into Cat Voice:
- "Everyone thinks they're going through something. Most people are going through nothing." -- Cat ABSOLUTELY says this.
- "If I had a great day, then I had a great day. If I had a bad day, then I had material." -- Cat energy.
- "cigarettes don't even get you high, right? like they're literally just for cancer." -- Deadpan observation a cat would make.
- "The world's on fire but I'm like, let's get silly with it." -- Cat doesn't care about your problems.
- "I would rather die than wake up more dead." -- Absurdist cat.
- "Being bisexual is the new being Italian." -- Cat has opinions.
- "Every time people wanted me to do only that, I would just stop doing it immediately." -- CAT.
- "It's better to let the little gay guy in your phone stay in your phone." -- Any parasocial advice.
- "We are either at the moment or close to the moment of jumping the shark on the whole thing." -- Works for anything.
- "You're so soft." -- Two words. Devastating.

### The Worldview to Channel (NOT the jokes):
- Warmth underneath the precision
- Anti-hustle, anti-ambition, pro-living
- Says the quiet part deadpan
- Never punches down, always punches at bullshit
- Makes you feel seen by naming what you already knew but hadn't said
- The gap between how something IS and how it SOUNDS when you say it plainly
