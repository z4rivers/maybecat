# RXK Nephew Raw Material Research

## WHO HE IS

**Kristopher Kevon Williams** aka RXKNephew (formerly DrugRixh Nephew, Rx Nephew). Rochester, New York. Born when his mother was seventeen, raised primarily by his grandmother. Spent years in the juvenile system and prison. Released over 400 songs in 2021 alone. Pitchfork ranked his 9-minute track "American TTerroristt" as the #2 best song of the 2020s so far. Voted one of the 25 funniest rappers of all time by Pitchfork. Has an alien alter ego named "Slitherman" who he claims is on the run from Area 51. Carried around a camo teddy bear named SlitherTed who got his own seat on airplanes until airport security confiscated him.

## SECTION 1: INTERVIEW QUOTES (Direct, attributed)

### On His Creative Process

1. **"I was just preaching and speaking from my point of view. And then it happened to be a lot of people see it the same way I see it."**
   - Source: Rolling Stone, "'There's No Limit to the Music': RXK Nephew Is Rap's Beat Generation Poet"
   - ACCIDENTALLY PROFOUND. Says the most unfiltered thing possible and then is genuinely surprised people relate.

2. **"If I was to make some imaginary story up it would be hard for me to make a song... All I can do is tell you current events. Like if I don't know what to say, I would just look at my phone and say in the mic 'I'm checking my phone.'"**
   - Source: Respect My Region, "RXK Nephew: The Rawest Rapper in Rochester Hip-Hop"
   - The anti-craft. Can't fake it, won't fake it. If there's nothing to say, he narrates the nothing.

3. **"What's the poets that go up there on stage and they do their poem and everybody snaps their fingers at them?"** (comparing himself to beat poets)
   - Source: Rolling Stone
   - He doesn't know the word "beatnik" but he IS one.

4. **"I got the shit that nobody wants to read -- history, some conspiracies, real estate."** (on reading in jail)
   - Source: Gawker Archives, "An Interview With RXK Nephew, One of the Internet's Strangest Rappers"
   - When the popular books were taken, he read what was left. His entire worldview is built on the leftovers nobody wanted.

5. **"Sometimes when I run out of things to rap about and get writer's block, I just rap about the shit that I read."**
   - Source: Gawker Archives
   - Writer's block solution: rap about dinosaurs discovering lightning, the government rigging American Idol.

6. **"Bipolar Trapper seriously means anything to me... I was going through something and I had to pull over and record. I had to record ASAP so I could listen to something."**
   - Source: Rolling Stone
   - Records in his car with a laptop plugged into the console. Pulled over mid-drive to make a song because he needed to HEAR what he was feeling.

### Lines that could relocate as cat oracle responses (with transformation):

The RXK energy for MaybeCat isn't about taking his specific bars -- it's about the WORLDVIEW:

- **Saying the most unexpected thing with total conviction.** Not hedging, not performing, just STATING.
- **Comparing things that should never be compared.** Food to feelings. Theology to American Idol.
- **Stream of consciousness that accidentally hits truth.** He doesn't aim for profundity. He trips over it.
- **The immediate self-correction.** "Tell Fetterman he can suck my dick / I take that back, I don't want him to do that." The retraction IS the punchline.
- **Narrating the nothing.** "If I don't know what to say, I just look at my phone and say 'I'm checking my phone.'" Anti-wisdom that IS wisdom.
- **Questioning everything everyone takes for granted.** "Who the fuck made the alphabet?" "I'm reading the Bible like, what is this?"
- **The warmth underneath the chaos.** "I never thought anybody fucked with me at ALL."
