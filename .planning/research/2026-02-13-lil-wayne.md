# Lil Wayne — Raw Material Research

## Research Method
Sourced from: Rolling Stone interviews (2018, 2025), Complex (35 Best Lines), REVOLT (25 bars), VIBE (social media captions), Vice ("A Year of Lil Wayne" series), Passion of the Weiss (unpublished interview), Billboard (Carter V lyrics), American Songwriter, HotNewHipHop, Songfacts, Wikipedia, Nicki Minaj tweets, Katie Couric CBS interview, Riverfront Times (interview quotes compilation), Abhay Kashyap pun analysis, Lil Wayne HQ lyrics database, and Solange "Mad" analysis.

**NO quote aggregator sites used.** (BrainyQuote, AZQuotes, Goodreads, QuoteFancy were found in search results but NOT used as primary sources.)

## SECTION 1: LYRICS — The Punchlines & Metaphors

### The Famous Ones (included because they genuinely work standalone)

1. **"Real G's move in silence like lasagna."**
   - Song: "6 Foot 7 Foot" (feat. Cory Gunz) — *Tha Carter IV* (2011)
   - The G in "lasagna" is silent. Simultaneously stupid and genius. The bar that launched a million debates.
   - **STANDALONE TEST: PASS** — Works as an answer about strategy, patience, stealth. "Should I announce my plans?" → this.

2. **"Safe sex is great sex, better wear a latex / Cuz you don't want that late text / that 'I think I'm late' text"**
   - Song: "Lollipop" (feat. Static Major) — *Tha Carter III* (2008)
   - Triple entendre: latex/late text/"I'm late" (pregnancy). The wordplay unfolds in layers.
   - **STANDALONE TEST: PARTIAL** — The "late text" pivot could work relocated, but needs the full setup.

3. **"Blind eyes could look at me and see the truth. Wonder if Stevie do?"**
   - Song: "Mr. Carter" (feat. Jay-Z) — *Tha Carter III* (2008)
   - The truth is so obvious even blindness can't hide it. Then the Stevie Wonder twist.
   - **STANDALONE TEST: PASS** — "Blind eyes could look at me and see the truth." Standalone, this is devastating.

4. **"I got some weight on my shoulders, to me it's like feathers."**
   - Song: "Right Above It" (feat. Drake) — *I Am Not a Human Being* (2010)
   - Reframes burden as nothing. Earned confidence.
   - **STANDALONE TEST: PASS** — Perfect answer to "Am I carrying too much?"

5. **"Motherf**ker, I'm ill, not sick / And I'm okay, but my watch sick."**
   - Song: "A Milli" — *Tha Carter III* (2008)
   - Ill (talented) vs. sick. Then the watch is "sick" (expensive/impressive). Wordplay cascade.
   - **STANDALONE TEST: PARTIAL** — "I'm ill, not sick" works as attitude. Full line needs context.

6. **"Stop playin', I do it like a king do / If these n***as animals, then I'ma have a mink soon."**
   - Song: "6 Foot 7 Foot" — *Tha Carter IV* (2011)

## SECTION 3: THE WAYNE ENERGY — What Makes It Work for MaybeCat

### Why Wayne Works as Source Material

1. **Metaphors that unfold** — Wayne's best lines have delayed comprehension. You hear it, pause, then it clicks. "Lasagna" is the most famous example but dozens more exist. This mirrors how a cat's answer could hit: you read it, move on, then 10 minutes later go "wait..."

2. **Profound while trying to be clever** — Wayne doesn't try to be deep. He's trying to be funny/hard/impressive, and accidentally says something that rewires your brain. This is exactly the MaybeCat voice: the cat isn't trying to help you. It's trying to be a cat. The wisdom is accidental.

3. **Confidence that's EARNED** — Wayne has the catalog to back every boast. This isn't empty hype. When he says "I'm the best," he has 20+ years of evidence. This translates to cat energy: the cat's confidence isn't delusional, it's feline. Cats ARE that good.

4. **Inverted reality** — "I am probably in the sky, flying with the fishes, or maybe in the ocean, swimming with the pigeons. See, my world is different." This is cat logic. Cats live in a different reality than you.

5. **Casual about chaos** — "I lost my mind, it's somewhere out there stranded." Said like he's mentioning he left his keys at the office. Catastrophe delivered as small talk. Peak cat energy.

6. **The rubber band theory of love** — His emotional observations hit because they use physical metaphors. "Love is like a rubber band. Both pull on. One releases. And it hurts the person who has held it." This is relocatable because it's IMAGE, not sentiment.

## SECTION 5: THE WAYNE WORDPLAY PATTERNS (for generating new material)

### Pattern 1: The Silent Letter
"Real G's move in silence like lasagna" — find words with silent letters and build meaning around the silence.

### Pattern 2: The Double Meaning Cascade
"I'm ill, not sick / And I'm okay, but my watch sick" — one word means two things, then cascades into a second double meaning.

### Pattern 3: The Physical Metaphor for Emotion
"Love is like a rubber band" — take an abstract feeling, make it a physical object, describe what happens to the object.

### Pattern 4: The Inverted Reality
"Flying with the fishes, swimming with the pigeons" — swap two things that don't belong. Declare your world different.

### Pattern 5: The Casual Catastrophe
"I lost my mind, it's somewhere out there stranded" — massive personal crisis described like a minor inconvenience.

### Pattern 6: The Impossible Timestamp
"A minute after never, so set your clocks" — invent a time/place/measurement that doesn't exist.

### Pattern 7: The Excellence Applied to Garbage
"When you throw trash in the garbage can, it has to be better than anybody else" — apply the highest standard to the lowest thing.

## SECTION 7: NOTES FOR RELOCATION SESSION

### What to bring to the batch:
- Wayne's **physical metaphors for emotions** (rubber band, feathers, stain) — these are found language that relocates cleanly
- Wayne's **casual catastrophe** delivery — crisis as small talk is peak cat energy
- Wayne's **earned confidence** — not empty boasting, backed by decades of proof (cats have millennia)
- Wayne's **inverted reality** lines — cats literally live in a different reality than humans
- Wayne's **impossible timestamps** — "a minute after never" is a cat concept. Cats don't do time.
- Wayne's **excellence applied to mundane things** — a cat making a simple jump look like Olympic gymnastics

### What does NOT relocate well:
- Pure braggadocio about money/women without wit
- Lines that require knowing the beat/flow to hit
- Wordplay that only works as wordplay (the "call that" puns are clever but don't answer questions)
- Context-dependent Katrina/prison/personal references
- The "silent letter" pattern is Wayne-specific and would feel like imitation

### The WORLDVIEW to capture (not the character):
Wayne sees the world as: **a place where effort is the only variable he controls, chaos is normal, love is physics not feelings, and being misunderstood is a feature not a bug.** This maps beautifully onto cat philosophy: effort = hunting, chaos = the house at 3AM, love = headbutts not words, misunderstood = every cat ever.
