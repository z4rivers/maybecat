# Drew Afualo -- Raw Material Research

## Who She Is
Drew Afualo (b. September 18, 1995) is a Samoan-American content creator, podcast host, comedian, and NYT bestselling author. Known as "the internet's tangible consequence" for misogynists. 8M+ combined TikTok/Instagram followers. Host of "The Comment Section" podcast (Spotify exclusive). Author of "Loud: Accept Nothing Less Than the Life You Deserve" (2024) -- first Samoan woman to appear on the NYT bestseller list. TIME100 Creators 2025. Forbes 30 Under 30 2025.

Her signature: devastating roasts delivered with genuine joy and an unmistakable high-pitched laugh that Bloomberg News describes as "a staccato, full-chested explosion of high-pitched emotion and outrage." She destroys misogynists while looking gorgeous doing it.

## THE DREW AFUALO ENERGY MAP (for MaybeCat relocation)

### What makes her voice WORK:
- **Joy in destruction.** She's not angry when she roasts -- she's DELIGHTED. The laugh is the weapon. She genuinely enjoys watching mediocre men realize they're mediocre.
- **Specificity kills.** Not "you're ugly" but "you look like you sell cell phones in a Sam's Club." The precision is the cruelty.
- **Confidence as default state.** She doesn't perform confidence. She doesn't argue for her worth. She starts from a place where her worth is so established that questioning it is the joke.
- **The reversal.** Men attack her looks, she attacks their entire existence. The asymmetry is the point.
- **"I'm not mean, you're just wrong."** She reframes every attack on her as evidence of the attacker's inadequacy, not hers.
- **Cultural grounding.** Her confidence comes from somewhere real -- Samoan family, heritage, ancestors. It's not aspirational influencer confidence. It's inherited.
- **The laugh as punctuation.** Every roast ends with a laugh that says "that was easy."

### The duality:
- PUBLIC: Devastating, unshakeable, laughing, joyful in destruction
- PRIVATE: "I am physically very unwell." "I consistently do not feel like enough."
- This duality is what makes her REAL -- the armor is real but it costs something
