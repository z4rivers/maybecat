# Friedensreich Hundertwasser — Found Language Research

**Date:** 2026-02-13
**Subject:** Friedensreich Regentag Dunkelbunt Hundertwasser (1928-2000), Austrian painter/architect/ecological activist
**Thesis:** Hundertwasser's philosophy is arguably the most catlike worldview ever committed to paper by a human. Anti-straight-line, anti-conformity, pro-comfort, pro-nature, fiercely independent, suspicious of authority, obsessed with beauty on his own terms. His ACTUAL words are the raw material.

## Source Key

All quotes below are traced to the most specific source I could verify. Sources ranked:

- **TIER 1 — Published manifesto/essay** (text available on hundertwasser.com or hundertwasser.at, or in published collections)
- **TIER 2 — Speech with documented occasion** (date, venue, context known)
- **TIER 3 — Published book attribution** (from *Hundertwasser Architecture*, Pierre Restany monograph, etc.)
- **TIER 4 — Museum/institutional attribution** (Hundertwasser Art Centre NZ, Kunst Haus Wien)
- **UNVERIFIED** — widely attributed but no primary source confirmed

I avoided quote aggregator sites (BrainyQuote, AZQuotes, QuoteFancy, etc.) as primary sources. When those sites were the ONLY source, the quote is marked UNVERIFIED.

## THE STRAIGHT LINE QUOTES

These are the crown jewels. Hundertwasser's lifelong war on the straight line is the most catlike philosophical position in Western art.

### 1. "The straight line is godless and immoral."
**Source:** "Mouldiness Manifesto against Rationalism in Architecture" (Verschimmelungsmanifest gegen den Rationalismus in der Architektur), first read at the Abbey of Seckau, Austria, July 4, 1958. Published as signed brochure by Galerie Renate Boukes, Wiesbaden, 1958. Later in Ulrich Conrads, *Programmes and Manifestos on Architecture of the 20th Century* (Berlin: Vieweg Verlag, 1964).
**TIER 1**
**CATLIKE:** 10/10. A cat has never walked in a straight line in its life.

### 2. "The straight line is not a creative line, it is a duplicating line, an imitating line."
**Source:** Same — Mouldiness Manifesto, 1958.
**TIER 1**

### 3. "Today we live in a chaos of straight lines, in a jungle of straight lines. If you do not believe this, take the trouble to count the straight lines which surround you. Then you will understand, for you will never finish counting."
**Source:** Same — Mouldiness Manifesto, 1958.
**TIER 1**
**CATLIKE:** 9/10. The dare energy. "Go ahead. Count them. I'll wait."

### 4. "Just carrying a ruler with you in your pocket should be forbidden, at least on a moral basis. The ruler is the symbol of the new illiteracy."
**Source:** Same — Mouldiness Manifesto, 1958. Also appears in "The Straight Line Leads to the Downfall of Our Civilisation" (1953 text, published later).
**TIER 1**
**CATLIKE:** 10/10. The absolute conviction that a common tool is morally offensive. Cat energy distilled.

### 5. "The straight line is the only uncreative line... The straight line is the true tool of the devil. Whosoever uses it is aiding the downfall of mankind."
**Source:** "Los von Loos" (Loose from Loos): A Law Permitting Individual Building Alterations, or Architecture-Boycott Manifesto. Read at Concordia Press Club, Vienna, February 9, 1968.
**TIER 1**
**CATLIKE:** 8/10. The escalation from "uncreative" to "tool of the devil" in one breath.

### 6. "The straight line leads to the downfall of humanity."
**Source:** Manifesto text "The Straight Line Leads to the Downfall of Our Civilisation" (Die gerade Linie fuhrt zum Untergang), concept from 1953, published text available on hundertwasser.com.
**TIER 1**
**CATLIKE:** 7/10. Stark. Would need trimming for cat voice but the conviction is there.

### 7. "The straight line is atheistic and immoral. The straight line is the only sterile line, the only line which does not suit man as the image of God. The straight line is the forbidden fruit."
**Source:** "On the Paradise Destroyed by the Straight Line" (Uber das durch die gerade Linie zerstorte Paradies), 1985.
**TIER 1**

### 8. "The cathedrals of true faith, the cathedrals of creation, cannot be built with straight lines, because the straight line is godless."
**Source:** "The Hamburg Line" (Die Linie von Hamburg), 1959. Related to the 10km continuous line painted at Lerchenfeld Art Institute, Hamburg.
**TIER 1**

## BONUS: THE NAME ITSELF

Hundertwasser changed his name multiple times. Born Friedrich Stowasser, he became:
- **Friedensreich** = "Peace-rich" or "Kingdom of Peace"
- **Regentag** = "Rainy day" (he loved rain)
- **Dunkelbunt** = "Dark-colorful" (added 1990s)
- **Hundertwasser** = "Hundred waters" (translation of Slavic root "sto" = hundred)

The act of renaming himself is itself catlike — cats have no use for the name you gave them.

## NOTES ON RELOCATION

The magic of Hundertwasser for MaybeCat:

**WHY THIS WORKS:**
- Hundertwasser's worldview IS a cat's worldview. He didn't need to be relocated — he was already speaking cat.
- His hatred of straight lines = a cat's natural navigation
- His insistence on beauty = a cat's aesthetic demands
- His contempt for conformity = a cat's refusal to comply
- His belief that the environment should serve the inhabitant = every cat ever
- His nude speeches = a cat is always nude

**RELOCATION APPROACHES:**
1. **Direct transplant** — Some quotes work as-is (e.g., "You are a guest of nature — behave." needs zero changes)
2. **Trim and sharpen** — Many of his longer passages contain a killer 5-10 word core buried in the manifesto language
3. **Twist the subject** — Replace "nature" with implied "me" (the cat). "You are a guest of [my space] — behave."
4. **Extract the worldview** — His ATTITUDE (absolute conviction about beauty, disgust at mediocrity, contempt for the expected) is the real material, even more than the specific words

**WHAT DOESN'T RELOCATE:**
- His political/ecological program language (too specific, too agenda-driven)
- The longer manifesto passages that need their full context
- Anything that requires knowing who Hundertwasser was to land

**BEST CATEGORIES FOR THESE:**
- demanding (the authority/judgment quotes)
- chaotic (the anti-straight-line fury)
- cryptic (the spiral/beauty/melody quotes)
- lucky (the permission/freedom quotes)
- unhinged (the shit manifesto, the nude speech energy)
