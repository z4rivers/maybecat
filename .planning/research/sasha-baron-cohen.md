## BORAT LINES

### The Catchphrases (Short, Punchy, Work as Oblique Answers)

- **"Very nice!"** — Said whenever he sees something (or someone) attractive. Universal approval.
- **"Great success!"** — Triumphant declaration after any minor achievement.
- **"Is nice!"** — Enthusiastic endorsement delivered with childlike wonder.
- **"High five!"** — Awkward celebration gesture. The energy of forced connection.
- **"My wife."** — Flat. Loaded. Could mean anything.
- **"Wawaweewa!"** — Pure shock/excitement with no filter.
- **"Very excite!"** — Uncontainable enthusiasm. Grammar be damned.
- **"I like!"** — Blunt. Honest. No elaboration needed.
- **"I like you. I like sex. Is nice."** — The entire human condition in 8 words.
- **"King in the castle! King in the castle! I have a chair!"** — Ecstatic about minor victories. Hotel room = kingdom.

### Deeper Borat Lines (Accidental Wisdom When Removed from Context)

- **"She is number-four prostitute in whole of Kazakhstan."** — Introducing his sister. Said with genuine pride. The energy of celebrating someone's modest ranking.
- **"He is pain in my assholes."** — On his neighbor. Pure, honest assessment. No diplomatic language.
- **"This-a my wife Oksana. She is-a boring."** — Devastating honesty about a relationship, delivered as casual fact.
- **"I feel like American movie star Dirty Harold."** — Confidence through misquotation. Getting the reference wrong but the energy right.
- **"Go ahead, make my day, Jew."** — Mangling Dirty Harry while holding a gun in a real gun shop. The shop owner just... helped him.
- **"In U.S. and A., if you want to marry a girl, you cannot just go to her father's house and swap her for 15 gallons of insecticide."** — Genuine bewilderment at dating customs.
- **"I arrive in America's airport with clothings, U.S. dollars, and a jar of gypsy tears to protect me from AIDS."** — Full confidence in a terrible plan.
- **"Although Kazakhstan a glorious country, it have a problem, too: economic, social, and Jew."** — Casual listing. The punchline is the delivery, not the content.

### Borat Rodeo Scene — Where the Crowd Cheered the Wrong Things

At a rodeo in Salem, Virginia. Borat addresses the crowd:
- **"We support your war of terror!"** — Crowd cheers.
- **"May we show our support to our boys in Iraq!"** — More cheers.
- **"May US and A kill every single terrorist!"** — Louder cheers.
- **"May your George Bush drink the blood of every single man, woman, and child of Iraq!"** — *Still cheering.* They didn't parse it. They heard "George Bush" and "Iraq" and kept clapping.
- **"May you destroy their country so that for next thousand years not even a single lizard will survive in their desert!"** — This is when some of them started to slow down.
- Then he sang a fake Kazakh national anthem to the tune of "The Star-Spangled Banner." Near-riot. Had to be escorted out by security.
- Rodeo producer Bobby Rowe: **"Had we not gotten them out of there, there would have been a riot."**

**THE INSIGHT:** The crowd agreed with everything UNTIL it was their anthem being mocked. Violent nationalism was fine. Disrespecting a song was the line.

### "Throw the Jew Down the Well" — The Bar Scene

At a honky-tonk bar in Tucson, Arizona. Borat plays acoustic guitar. Performs "In My Country There Is Problem."
- A country-style song with three verses: first about transport problems, second directed at Jews, third warning "be careful of his teeth."
- **The crowd started singing along.** Chanting "Throw the Jew Down the Well." Some audience members started miming horns (devil horns on Jews).
- The performance lasted two and a half hours.
- Baron Cohen, who is Jewish, has stated the purpose was to **"expose antisemitism in his interview subjects, as well as simple indifference to antisemitism."**
- The Anti-Defamation League sent Cohen an open letter saying they understood his intent but were "concerned that this aspect may not have been grasped by his audience."

**THE INSIGHT:** People will sing along to ANYTHING if the melody is catchy and the crowd is doing it. Indifference is the real danger.

### Gun Shop Scene

Borat walks into a real gun store. Asks what's the best gun to defend himself from Jews.
- **The gun shop owner recommends a 9mm or a .45.**
- Didn't question the premise. Just answered the question.
- Borat picks up a gold, tiger-striped Desert Eagle and says: **"I feel like American movie star Dirty Harold!"**
- Couldn't buy it because he wasn't a U.S. citizen. So he bought a bear instead.

### The Etiquette Dinner

Borat takes etiquette lessons from a real instructor. Gets invited to a dinner party.
- Arrives. Tries to follow the rules. Eventually brings a bag of his own excrement to the table.
- Then invites a sex worker to the party.
- The dinner guests' reactions were real. The etiquette instructor later sued (thrown out).

### The Frat Boys in the RV

Borat hitchhikes. Gets picked up by three fraternity brothers from University of South Carolina.
- Production crew took them to a bar first to "loosen up."
- On camera, the frat boys made racist and sexist comments about slavery and minorities.
- They later sued, claiming: **"The production crew plied them with alcohol"** and they **"engaged in behavior that they otherwise would not have engaged in."**
- **Judge threw it out.** Saw right through the "alcohol made me say it" defense.
- Their names: Christopher Rotunda and Justin Seay.

**THE INSIGHT:** Alcohol doesn't create opinions. It reveals them.

## BORAT 2 (SUBSEQUENT MOVIEFILM)

### Rudy Giuliani Hotel Room Scene

Maria Bakalova (playing Tutar, Borat's daughter) secures a private interview with Rudy Giuliani at a hotel. She's posing as a conservative journalist.
- Giuliani follows her into a bedroom. Places his hands down his pants.
- Sacha Baron Cohen bursts in as Borat: **"She's 15! She's too old for you!"**
- Giuliani's defense: **"I was merely tucking my shirt back into my pants."**
- Cohen at the Golden Globes: **"I've gotta say this movie couldn't have been possible without my co-star, a fresh new talent who came from nowhere and turned out to be a comedy genius. I'm talking, of course, about Rudy Giuliani."**
- Bakalova: **"It's going to show Rudy's real character. You're responsible for your own decisions. So, no, I don't feel bad."**
- Bakalova also said: **"Movies like this are showing people's true colors."**

### Jeanise Jones — The Babysitter Who Became the Moral Center

62-year-old Jeanise Jones from Oklahoma was hired to babysit Tutar. She didn't know it was a movie.
- When told Tutar was being groomed for plastic surgery by her father:
  - **"You shouldn't want to be anyone else, but yourself."**
  - **"Use your brain, because your daddy is a liar."**
  - **"I don't think you need to do that because you're pretty and you're young, and any man should like you as you are."**
- She was paid $3,600. When she found out the truth: **"I can't say it was fair because they knew it was going to be a movie, and I didn't."**
- Fans raised $180,000 on GoFundMe for her. Cohen donated $100,000 to her community.
- She said she felt **"betrayed"** but also became the film's unexpected hero.

**THE INSIGHT:** The most honest person in the entire film was the one who wasn't acting.

### Judith Dim Evans — Holocaust Survivor at the Synagogue

Borat enters a synagogue "disguised as a typical Jew" — long fake Pinocchio nose, bag of money, bat wings on his back.
- Meets Judith Dim Evans, a Holocaust survivor. She was told she'd be in a documentary.
- Borat: **"Very nice weather we have been controlling."** (Jewish weather control conspiracy, played straight)
- Borat: **"But the Holocaust didn't happen."**
- Evans: **"I saw it with my own eyes."**
- Borat: **"The Holocaust happened, really?!"**
- Evans tells him her story. Borat confesses he is **"very depressed."** She gives him a hug and a kiss.
- Cohen broke his own rule: he had someone tell Evans afterward that Borat was played by a Jewish actor promoting Holocaust education. He dedicated the film to her.
- Evans' estate sued. Dismissed.

**THE INSIGHT:** Kindness doesn't need context. She comforted a stranger wearing a hate costume.

### The Debutante Ball — "Moon Blood" Scene

Borat takes Tutar to a real debutante ball. Attendees were paid $100 as extras, told it was a coming-of-age film.
- Tutar whispers to Borat that she's on her "moon blood" (period).
- Borat: **"Now we can wow them with our traditional fertility dance."**
- They perform a synchronized dance. Crowd claps along.
- Tutar hikes her dress up to expose period-stained underwear.
- **"The energy of the room quickly changed from joviality to shock and disgust."** People stormed out.

### The Plastic Surgeon

Borat consults with Dr. Charles Wallace of Dallas about breast implants for Tutar, and making sure she doesn't look Jewish.
- Borat uses his hand to show a "Jew nose." The surgeon responds: **"It can be that bad, yes."**
- When Tutar asks if the surgeon would "make a sex attack" on her, Wallace pauses, then says: **"If your father was not here."**

### The Anti-Mask Rally (COVID-era)

At a "March for Our Rights" gun rally in Olympia, Washington, during COVID:
- Borat performs "The Wuhan Flu" — lyrics include: **"Mask wearers, what we going to do? Inject them with the Wuhan flu"** and **"Journalists, what we going to do? Slice them up like the Saudis do."**
- **The crowd sang along.**
- When they recognized him, they turned violent. Security held them off while Cohen raced to a getaway car.
- He was wearing a bulletproof vest. **"I was pretty lucky to get out this time."**

### COVID Lockdown with QAnon Conspiracy Theorists

Borat quarantines with two men — Jim Russell and Jerry Holleman — in Washington State.
- They're genuine QAnon believers who help him while sharing conspiracy theories.
- Amazon later released "Borat's American Lockdown" — Jim and Jerry's theories were debunked by experts on camera.
- The men were earnest. They believed what they believed. They were also hospitable.

## ALI G LINES

### Best Ali G Interview Moments

**Buzz Aldrin (astronaut):**
- Ali G calls him **"Buzz Lightyear."**
- Ali G: "Was you ever jealous of Louis Armstrong?" (Meaning Neil Armstrong.)
- Aldrin has to correct him that it was NEIL Armstrong who landed on the Moon.
- Ali G asks what will happen "when a man walks on the Sun."
- Ali G asks if "the people on the Moon" were friendly or scared when Aldrin landed.
- Ali G: **"What do you say to all of those conspiracy theorists who come up to you and say, does the Moon really exist?"**

**Noam Chomsky (linguist):**

- Tries to get Chomsky to explain complex linguistic theory in simple terms.

**Pat Buchanan (political commentator):**
- Ali G: **"'Ow many times was you President?"**
- Confuses WMDs with BLTs: **"Is it ever worth fighting a war over sandwiches?"** Buchanan almost takes the bait, thinking he means "bio-lethal toxins."

**Sam Donaldson (journalist):**
- Ali G asks if he remembers **"the scandal of Waterworld"** (meaning Watergate).
- Confuses it with the Kevin Costner movie AND Stargate.

**Boutros Boutros-Ghali (UN Secretary General):**
- Ali G calls him **"Boutros Boutros Boutros Ghali."** Adds an extra Boutros.

**Newt Gingrich, James Baker (Secretary of State):**

**Andy Rooney (60 Minutes):**

**Donald Trump:**
- Ali G pitches Trump a business idea: **gloves for eating ice cream** so your hands don't get sticky.
- Ali G: "What do you think is the most popular thing in the world?" Trump: "Music." Ali G: "No... ice cream."

### Ali G in Parliament

- **"You wanna know 'ow I make diz country bettah? Iz simple, two words: keep it real."**
- **"I put it to you that you sucked off an 'orse."** — To an actual politician.

**THE INSIGHT:** Ali G's genius is that his stupid questions force smart people to explain basic things — and sometimes they can't. The dumb question is the diagnostic tool.

## "WHO IS AMERICA" — THE EXPOSED MOMENTS

### The "Kinderguardians" Segment

Cohen disguised as Israeli anti-terror expert Col. Erran Morad pitches a fake program to arm children as young as 3 with "gunimals" (guns with soft toy decorations).

**Politicians who endorsed it ON CAMERA:**

- **Trent Lott** (former Senate Republican Leader): **"I support the Kinderguardians program. We in America would be wise to implement it, too."**
- **Dana Rohrabacher** (CA Congressman): **"Maybe having young children trained and understand how to defend themselves in their school may actually make us safer here."**
- **Joe Wilson** (SC Congressman): **"A three-year-old cannot defend themselves from an assault rifle by throwing a Hello Kitty pencil case at it. Our founding fathers did not put an age limit on the Second Amendment."**
- **Larry Pratt** (Gun Owners of America executive director): **"Toddlers are pure, uncorrupted by fake news or homosexuality. They don't care if it's politically correct to shoot a mentally deranged gunman."**

Rohrabacher later denied it: **"At no time did I endorse training toddlers in handling guns."** Called it **"fraud, a sick fraud at that."**

### Jason Spencer — Georgia State Representative

Cohen (as Col. Morad) convinces Spencer to do "anti-terrorism training."
- Spencer speaks in a mock Asian accent while pretending to take upskirt photos under a woman's burqa.
- Cohen tells him yelling a forbidden word will attract attention during a kidnapping. **Spencer screams the N-word repeatedly.**
- Cohen tells him Muslim extremists are religiously repulsed by bare buttocks. **Spencer drops his pants, charges backward with his bare ass out, shouting "USA!" and "America!"**
- Spencer's statement afterward: **"This ridiculously ugly episode"** — blamed the show's producers for exploiting him "for profit and notoriety."
- **Spencer resigned from the Georgia House of Representatives.**

### Roy Moore — The Pedophile Detector

Cohen (as Col. Morad) tells former Alabama Senate candidate Roy Moore about Israeli technology that can detect pedophiles through enzyme secretion.
- Cohen: **"Sex offenders and particularly pedophiles secrete an enzyme, DDHT, which is actually detectable."**
- The "detector" beeps when it passes near Moore. Moore sits stone-faced.
- Moore: **"I've been married for 33 years, I've never had an accusation of such things. If this is an instrument, then certainly I'm not a pedophile."**
- Moore: **"I am simply cutting this conversation right now."** Walks off.
- Moore sued for $95 million. Statement: **"I am involved in several court cases presently to defend my honor and character against vicious false political attacks by liberals like Cohen."**
- **Cohen won. The lawsuit was dismissed.**

## THE OFFENDED — Real Reactions from Real People

### Kazakhstan Government

- **Kazakh spokesperson Roman Vassilenko (2005):** "Mr. Cohen could not have been more wrong when he chose Kazakhstan as a home country for his mythical, misogynist and anti-Semitic reporter. **I am offended and the people of Kazakhstan are offended by the choice.**"
- Kazakhstan hired two Western PR firms and took out a **four-page ad in the New York Times** to debunk Borat myths.
- **Foreign Minister Yerzhan Kazykhanov (2012), complete reversal:** "I am grateful to 'Borat' for helping attract tourists to Kazakhstan." Visa applications had risen tenfold.
- For Borat 2 (2020), Kazakhstan adopted "Very nice!" as their **official tourism slogan.**

**THE ARC:** Offense -> PR campaign -> grudging acceptance -> actual gratitude -> adopting the catchphrase.

### The Romanian Village of Glod

The village of Glod (1,670 Roma residents) was used as a stand-in for Kazakhstan. Residents were told it was a documentary about poverty.
- **Dana Luca, resident:** "We thought they came here to help us — not mock us."
- **Other resident:** "They made us put a cow in our living room, and they made it defecate and urinate in the house. Everyone's angry because they didn't pay them the way they should have. They're making a lot of money — but they've made us a laughing stock."
- Paid $3.30 to $5.50 per person.
- **Nicolae Staicu, community leader:** Led a $30 million lawsuit against Cohen and 20th Century Fox.
- ABC News headline: **"If I See Borat, I Will Kill Him With My Own Hands"**
- **Lawsuit thrown out** by Judge Loretta Preska — charges "too vague and nebulous."

### Pamela Anderson

## SACHA BARON COHEN — OUT OF CHARACTER

### On His Comedy Philosophy

- **"Borat essentially works as a tool. By himself being anti-Semitic, he lets people lower their guard and expose their own prejudice."** — Rolling Stone interview, ~2006
- **"People really let down their guard with [Borat] because they're in a room with somebody who seems to have these outrageous opinions. They sometimes feel much more relaxed about letting their own outrageous, politically incorrect, prejudiced opinions come out."** — NPR interview
- **"As a comedian, I've tried to use my characters to get people to let down their guard and reveal what they actually believe, including their own prejudice."** — ADL speech, 2019
- **"They have a camera in front of them and they can reveal themselves to either be horrific or good or a mixture."** — NPR Fresh Air, 2021
- **"Thank you to every American who has not sued me so far."** — Golden Globe acceptance speech, 2007
- **"Thank you to the all-white Hollywood Foreign Press."** — Golden Globe acceptance speech, 2021

### On Ethics and Purpose

- **"Those are the discussions that we have in the writers' room continually: Is this ethical? What's the purpose of this scene? Is it just to be funny? Is there some satire? Is that satire worth it?"** — NPR, 2021
- **"We did it because there was a deeply unethical government in power. We had to do what we could to inspire people to vote and remind people of the immorality of the government prior to the election. I have no doubt about the morality of this film."** — On Borat 2
- **"I think the answer is Donald Trump. I remember the Muslim ban and friends sending me articles. And at that point, I think I was just so angry that I felt I had to do something other than forward articles in The New York Times and Washington Post."** — On why he revived Borat
- **"The path to Auschwitz was paved with indifference."** — On the real lesson

### The ADL Speech (2019) — Baron Cohen Goes Full Serious

Receiving the Anti-Defamation League's International Leadership Award:
- **"All this hate and violence is being facilitated by a handful of internet companies that amount to the greatest propaganda machine in history."**
- **"The algorithms these platforms depend on deliberately amplify the type of content that keeps users engaged — stories that appeal to our baser instincts and that trigger outrage and fear."**
- **"It's why YouTube recommended videos by the conspiracist Alex Jones billions of times. It's why fake news outperforms real news, because studies show that lies spread faster than truth."**
- **"If Facebook were around in the 1930s, it would have allowed Hitler to post 30-second ads on his 'solution' to the 'Jewish problem.'"**
- **"If we prioritize truth over lies, tolerance over prejudice... we can still have a place for free speech and free expression. And, most importantly, my jokes will still work."**
- Called tech CEOs **"high-tech robber barons."**

### On Physical Danger

- **"Under my overalls, I was wearing a bulletproof vest, but it felt inadequate with some people outside toting semiautomatic weapons."**
- **"When someone ripped open the door to drag me out, I used my entire body weight to pull the door back shut until our vehicle manoeuvred free."**

### Background

- Jewish. Created an anti-Semitic character specifically to expose anti-Semitism.
- **"Dictators are ludicrous characters, and in my career and in my life, I've always enjoyed sort of inhabiting these ludicrous, larger-than-life characters that somehow exist in the real world."**

## PATTERNS FOR MAYBECAT MINING

### The "Accidentally Wise" Template
Lines that sound stupid but contain real truth when applied to a different question:
- "Very nice!" (universal approval with zero context)
- "Great success!" (celebrating with no basis)
- "I like!" (honest, no explanation needed)
- "Is nice!" (childlike enthusiasm)
- "My wife." (the flattest possible acknowledgment)
- "He is pain in my assholes." (honest relationship assessment)
- "King in the castle! I have a chair!" (celebrating tiny wins)
- "She is-a boring." (devastating honesty)
- "High five!" (forced connection)
- "Pointy is scary." (accidental wisdom about design and fear)

### The "Exposed by the Stupid Question" Template
The cat asks something dumb that accidentally reveals the real answer:
- "Is it cos I is Black?" (deflection that works on everything)
- "Is it ever worth fighting a war over sandwiches?" (accidentally profound)
- "Does the Moon really exist?" (existential via stupidity)
- "What do you think is the most popular thing in the world?" / "Ice cream." (confidence in a wrong answer)
- "Fashion saves a lot more lives than doctors." (said with total conviction)

### The "Offended People Reveal Themselves" Template
The reaction to the prank IS the content:
- Gun shop owner recommending a 9mm to defend against Jews
- Bar crowd singing "Throw the Jew Down the Well"
- Frat boys blaming alcohol for their racism
- Politicians endorsing arming toddlers
- Jason Spencer screaming the N-word and showing his bare ass for "anti-terrorism"
- Rodeo crowd cheering "drink the blood of every man, woman, and child"

### The "Kindness Needs No Context" Template
Moments where genuine human goodness shone through:
- Jeanise Jones telling a stranger's daughter she's worth more
- Judith Dim Evans hugging a man in an anti-Semitic costume
- The driving instructor patiently teaching Borat to drive
- Kazakhstan eventually saying "thank you"
