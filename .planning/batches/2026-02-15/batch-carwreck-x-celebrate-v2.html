v2 — rebuilt from real crash language. Sourced from Reddit survivors, Quora first-person accounts, NPR, psychology research on time distortion, actual insurance claims, first responders. NOT vocabulary words. What it FELT like.
The collision: Time slowing to honey. Glass spraying like a fan and looking beautiful. The weird peace. The music still playing. "Am I dead?" for three days after. Hands intertwined when they woke up. Celebration language meets that. Word tapestries, not label pairs.

Raw Research Gems (scroll)

Time Distortion:

• "Everything seemed to go into slow motion. There was a very long gap between the sound of impact and the beginning of the car's spin." • "I was surprised by how clear and vivid everything became. There was a strange sense of quietness too." • "The event happened perhaps in a second or two, but it felt much longer." — neuroscientist Marc Wittmann on his own skid • 71% of people recall altered passage of time during accidents

The Glass:

• "The glass sprayed out so slowly, like a fan, and it looked beautiful. All the pieces were shining in the sun." • "I felt like I was floating through the air, almost as if I wasn't going to come down."

The Weird Calm:

• Dax Shepard: "And the feeling was shockingly comforting." • "I felt oddly at peace." — person who bled out and was resuscitated • "A peculiar sense of peace nestled in the shattering glass and contorted metal."

The Music Still Playing:

• Entire forum thread of people sharing what song was playing during their crash • "I arrived on scene first and the radio was going in the Honda, with a kids' CD playing." • After flipping his car, a driver turned UP the volume on AC/DC's "Highway to Hell"

"Am I Dead?":

• "For the next three days, I'd just be places and I felt very peculiar like, 'Am I dead?'" • "Now things, people and places are not how I remember or know them to be."

Insurance Claims (real):

• "An invisible car came out of nowhere, struck my vehicle, and vanished." • "The accident was caused by me waving to the man I hit last week." • "Leaving home I drove straight into a bus; the bus was five minutes early." • Warning given by other party: "Moo."
