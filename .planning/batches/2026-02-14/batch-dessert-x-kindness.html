Refrigerators: 5-star dessert recipe reviews (the purple prose of home bakers) × random acts of kindness lists (the earnest instructions of good people). The collision barely makes sense. That’s the point.
Why it works: Recipe reviews talk about food like it’s a religious experience (“sinfully rich,” “died and went to heaven,” “devoured in minutes”). Kindness lists give precise instructions for goodness (“slip a note,” “tape a quarter,” “scatter everywhere”). When a cat mashes these together, you get instructions that sound like either dessert recipes, life advice, or both. Body parts included where they help.
